Portadas / Cover pages
----------------------
Las portadas (*-coverpage.md), salvo Mutual-NDA-coverpage.md, proceden de las
versiones en Word que Common Paper publica en https://commonpaper.com/standards
(la URL exacta figura al final de cada fichero). Pre-Legal las ha convertido a
Markdown: el texto de Common Paper no se ha modificado, pero sí el formato (tablas
convertidas en títulos, listas de casillas y notas). Las notas marcadas como
«Pre-Legal note» son añadidos de Pre-Legal. sla-coverpage.md es la fila del SLA de
la portada del CSA 2.1 con SLA.

- AI-Addendum-coverpage.md                 <- ai-addendum/1.0 (solo la portada)
- BAA-coverpage.md                         <- business-associate-agreement/1.0
- CSA-coverpage.md                         <- cloud-service-agreement/2.1
- design-partner-agreement-coverpage.md    <- design-partner-agreement/1.3
- DPA-coverpage.md                         <- data-processing-agreement/1.1
- Partnership-Agreement-coverpage.md       <- partnership-agreement/1.0
- Pilot-Agreement-coverpage.md             <- pilot-agreement/1.1
- psa-coverpage.md                         <- professional-services-agreement/1.0 (solo la portada)
- sla-coverpage.md                         <- cloud-service-agreement/2.1 (portada con SLA)
- Software-License-Agreement-coverpage.md  <- software-license-agreement/1.1

The cover pages (*-coverpage.md), except Mutual-NDA-coverpage.md, come from the Word
versions published by Common Paper at https://commonpaper.com/standards (the exact
URL is at the end of each file). Pre-Legal converted them to Markdown: Common Paper's
text is unchanged, but the formatting was adapted (tables turned into headings,
checkbox lists and notes). Notes marked "Pre-Legal note" were added by Pre-Legal.

Traducciones al español / Spanish translations
----------------------------------------------
El directorio Templates/es/ contiene traducciones no oficiales al español de las
plantillas anteriores, realizadas por Pre-Legal. Son obras derivadas: el texto ha
sido modificado respecto al original (traducción), se distribuyen también bajo
CC BY 4.0 y mantienen la atribución a Common Paper. Cada traducción lo indica al
principio del documento. No son traducciones oficiales de Common Paper ni adaptan
los acuerdos al derecho español. Templates/es/GLOSARIO.md recoge los criterios y
términos usados.

The Templates/es/ directory contains unofficial Spanish translations of the
templates above, made by Pre-Legal. They are adapted material: the text has been
modified from the original (translation), they are also licensed under CC BY 4.0
and retain the attribution to Common Paper. Each translation says so at the top.
They are not official Common Paper translations and do not adapt the agreements
to Spanish law.
