# Pilot Agreement

## USING THE ORDER FORM

The Agreement has 2 parts: (1) the Order Form below (including any attached or referenced policies and documents) and (2) the Common Paper Pilot Agreement Standard Terms v1.1 posted at [https://commonpaper.com/standards/pilot-agreement/1.1](https://commonpaper.com/standards/pilot-agreement/1.1) which are incorporated by reference. If there is any inconsistency between the Order Form and the Standard Terms, the Order Form will control for that inconsistency. Capitalized words have the meanings or descriptions given in the Order Form or Standard Terms. A copy of the Standard Terms is attached for convenience only.
